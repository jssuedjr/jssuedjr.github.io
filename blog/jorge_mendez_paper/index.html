Inline Mathematics

Suppose that $X$ is a random variable with mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$.

We can write its expected value as $\mathbb{E}[X] = \mu$.

Display Mathematics

For a continuous random variable,

$$ \mathbb{E}[X] = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x f_X(x)\,dx. $$

Similarly, the variance is

$$ \operatorname{Var}(X) = \mathbb{E}\left[(X-\mu)^2\right]. $$

A More Interesting Example

If

$$ X \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu,\sigma^2), $$

then its probability density function is

$$ f_X(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2}} \exp\left( -\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2} \right). $$

That's pretty cool.
